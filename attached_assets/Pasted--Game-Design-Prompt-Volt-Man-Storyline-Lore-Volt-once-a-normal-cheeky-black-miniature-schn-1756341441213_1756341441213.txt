🎮 Game Design Prompt: Volt-Man
📖 Storyline / Lore

Volt, once a normal cheeky black miniature schnauzer, is struck by mystic lightning fused with ancient runes (Image 1 reference: glowing rune paw + lightning bolt).

He transforms into Volt-Man (Image 2 & 4 references: Volt standing upright with glowing runes, headphones, harness).

Mutant rabbits (corrupted by shadow magic) now infest the Maze of Realms, an interdimensional labyrinth.

Volt must dodge, outsmart, and zap the rabbits while collecting glowing treats that grant temporary power-up form (Image 2 reference: Volt radiating energy with skull flames).

With the power-up active, Volt can chomp the mutant rabbits for bonus points.

Goal: survive each maze, clear all treats, rack up points, and unlock new levels while unraveling Volt’s journey through the Rune Realms.

🎮 Core Gameplay Mechanics

Classic Pac-Man style maze gameplay:

Volt-Man moves through mazes eating small glowing “treats.”

Collect all treats to complete the level.

Enemies: Mutant Rabbits

Move through the maze chasing Volt.

Contact = Volt loses a life.

Power-Up Treats:

Larger glowing biscuits/bones.

Eating one gives Volt temporary power (X seconds).

Rabbits turn “edible” (blue glowing, scared state).

Scoring System:

Treat = 10 points.

Rabbit eaten under power-up = 200, 400, 800, 1600 (classic stacking system).

Bonus pickups (glowsticks, bass speakers, skull charms) = 1000 points.

🎨 Art & Visual Style

Reference Images Provided (use these as base):

Image 1: Rune pawprint + lightning (intro logo, loading screens, runic UI elements).

Image 2: Volt standing upright, rune-powered (power-up Volt form).

Image 3: Volt in semi-normal powered state (normal gameplay avatar).

Image 4: Volt-Man in harness/headphones glowing rune chest (main character select + intro art).

Style: Neon blue cyber-occult runes + glowing aura, dark backgrounds, clean glowing mazes.

Maze Design:

Neon-outlined walls (electric blue, cyan, occasional purple accents).

Stars / rune glyphs faintly glowing in the background.

Enemies (Mutant Rabbits):

Small but creepy glowing rabbits with rune-corruption.

Default: glowing red eyes.

Power-up state: glowing blue, scared expression.

🔊 Music & Sound

Core Style:

Retro Gameboy chiptune vibe, but mixed with modern sound design (clean synths, deep bass, crispy hi-hats).

Tempo: ~130 BPM (arcade energetic).

Tracks:

Intro Theme: Heroic retro-electro track with rising synths.

Maze Loop: Classic bouncy chiptune with modern basslines (repeatable, ~60 sec loop).

Power-Up Theme: Faster, glitchier beat with sub-heavy drops.

Game Over Theme: Short descending arpeggio with glitch FX.

SFX:

Volt eating treat = crunchy chomp.

Volt power-up = bass drop + electric spark.

Rabbit eaten = distorted squeak/pop.

Losing life = glitchy zap + low bass tone.

🖥️ Interface / UX

Intro Screen:

Glowing Rune Paw (Image 1) appears → lightning strike → Volt-Man logo appears.

Menu Options:

“Start Game”

“High Scores”

“Options” (music toggle, difficulty).

In-Game HUD:

Score top-left.

Lives (Volt paw icons) bottom-left.

Level indicator top-right.

Pause Menu: clean neon overlay.

🧩 Levels & Progression

Level 1: Basic neon maze, 2 rabbits.

Level 2+: Increasing complexity (more corridors, more rabbits).

Every 3 levels: New maze design (rotating rune patterns in background).

Difficulty scaling:

More rabbits.

Faster rabbit speed.

Shorter power-up duration.

Possible Bonus Rounds:

“Treat Frenzy” → all treats are bonus points, no enemies.

⚙️ Technical Specs for Development

Platform: Android.

Frameworks: Unity (C#), Godot (GDScript), or simple HTML5 wrapper via Replit (JS/Canvas).

Controls:

Swipe gestures (mobile).

On-screen joystick optional.

Performance Priority:

Smooth 60 FPS.

Low CPU drain (optimize neon glow via shaders).

Scaling: Responsive resolution support (phones + tablets).

Stable Pac-Man clone style gameplay with Volt-Man reskin.

3–5 levels.

High Score tracking.

Power-Up mechanic fully functional.

Clean UI + menu system.

All music/SFX integrated.

No bugs, smooth input.

Boss Rabbits (giant mutant rabbit every 10 levels).

Rune unlocks (Volt gains new aura effects).

Multiplayer chase (online Volt vs Volt).

Music-reactive mazes (bass drops pulse maze walls).
